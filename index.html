Privacy Policy for PDF Unlocker

Last Updated: October 24, 2025

Introduction

PDF Unlocker ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Android application.

TL;DR (Summary)

  • We collect ZERO personal data
  • Your PDFs never leave your device
  • No internet connection required
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads
  • Completely private and secure

Information We Do NOT Collect

PDF Unlocker does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any of the following:

  • ❌ Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • ❌ PDF files or their contents
  • ❌ Passwords entered into the app
  • ❌ Device information
  • ❌ Usage data or analytics
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Contact information
  • ❌ Photos, videos, or other media
  • ❌ Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, etc.)
  • ❌ IP address
  • ❌ Browsing history
  • ❌ Any other personal data

How the App Works

PDF Unlocker processes all files locally on your device:

  1. You select a password-protected PDF from your device storage
  2. You enter the password
  3. The app processes the file entirely on your device
  4. The unlocked PDF is saved to your chosen location on your device
  5. No data is ever transmitted over the internet

Permissions Explained

Storage Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

Why: To read password-protected PDF files and save unlocked versions
Usage: Only files you explicitly select are accessed
Scope: Limited to PDF files you choose through the file picker

We do NOT:

  • Scan or access other files on your device
  • Upload files to any server
  • Share files with third parties
  • Store or cache files

Data Storage

  • Passwords: NOT stored. Passwords are used in memory only during the unlocking process and are immediately discarded
  • PDF Files: Processed in memory, NOT stored by the app
  • Preferences: The app may store minimal local settings (e.g., theme preference) using Android's SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device
  • Cache: Temporary files may be created during processing but are deleted immediately after

Third-Party Services

PDF Unlocker does NOT use any third-party services, including:

  • ❌ No analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)
  • ❌ No advertising networks
  • ❌ No crash reporting tools
  • ❌ No cloud services
  • ❌ No social media integrations
  • ❌ No payment processors (app is free)

Internet Access

PDF Unlocker does not require or request internet access. The app works completely offline.

Children's Privacy

PDF Unlocker does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. The app is safe for all ages.

Open Source

PDF Unlocker uses the following open-source libraries:

  • Apache PDFBox - Licensed under Apache License 2.0
  • These libraries process PDFs locally and do not transmit data

Data Security

Since we don't collect any data, there's no data to be breached. However, we implement security best practices:

  • All PDF processing happens in local memory
  • Passwords are not logged or stored
  • Files are accessed only through Android's secure Storage Access Framework (SAF)
  • No network communications
