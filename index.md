## How the App Works

PDF Unlocker processes all files **locally on your device**:

1. You select a password-protected PDF from your device storage
2. You enter the password
3. The app processes the file **entirely on your device**
4. The unlocked PDF is saved to your chosen location **on your device**
5. **No data is ever transmitted over the internet**

## Permissions Explained

PDF Unlocker requires the following Android permissions:

### Storage Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)
**Why:** To read password-protected PDF files and save unlocked versions
**Usage:** Only files you explicitly select are accessed
**Scope:** Limited to PDF files you choose through the file picker

**We do NOT:**
- Scan or access other files on your device
- Upload files to any server
- Share files with third parties
- Store or cache files

## Data Storage

- **Passwords:** NOT stored. Passwords are used in memory only during the unlocking process and are immediately discarded
- **PDF Files:** Processed in memory, NOT stored by the app
- **Preferences:** The app may store minimal local settings (e.g., theme preference) using Android's SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device
- **Cache:** Temporary files may be created during processing but are deleted immediately after
